How to prepare USDT in Futures wallet

  1. 1Buy or deposit USDT in Binance. P2P users can go to Trade, P2P, Express, choose USDT, then complete payment.
  2. 2Open Wallet or Futures, then click Transfer.
  3. 3Select transfer from Spot/Funding to USD-M Futures.
  4. 4Select USDT, enter at least 50 USDT, and confirm.
